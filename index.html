1. Who We Are

Garda is a streaming application that provides video entertainment content. All videos available in the Garda app are from Sudha Films. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Garda app on Android and iOS platforms.

2. Video Content from Sudha Films

The Garda app streams video content supplied by Sudha Films. Watching this content may generate usage data such as titles played, playback progress, and related in-app activity so we can operate the service. Sudha Films remains the source of the videos shown in the app.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account and contact information (if you sign up), such as name, mobile number, and email address.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and unique identifiers.
  • Usage information, such as pages viewed, watch history for Sudha Films videos, search terms, and in-app interactions.
  • Payment-related details for subscriptions, processed securely by trusted payment providers (we do not store full card details).

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve streaming of Sudha Films videos.
  • To personalize recommendations and user experience.
  • To manage subscriptions, billing, and customer support.
  • To detect fraud, security incidents, and misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share limited data with:

  • Cloud hosting and analytics providers.
  • Payment gateways and subscription management partners.
  • Customer support and communication service providers.
  • Sudha Films, as needed to deliver and support video content.
  • Authorities, when required by law or legal process.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Permissions Used by the App

Garda may request permissions such as internet/network access, notifications, and storage/media access (where required) only to support core app features like streaming Sudha Films videos, downloads (if enabled), and service updates. You can manage permissions in your device settings at any time.

7. Legal Basis and Compliance

We process your information based on one or more of the following: your consent, performance of our service agreement, legitimate business interests, and compliance with legal obligations. We follow applicable privacy laws and platform requirements, including Google Play and Apple App Store policies.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal requirements. When no longer needed, we delete or anonymize data where possible.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Garda is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal data. To request support, contact us at: official.garda@garda.com.

12. Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services (such as analytics, payment, crash reporting, or content delivery partners) that process information on our behalf to help us run and improve Garda. Video content is provided by Sudha Films. These providers are expected to protect your data and use it only for authorized purposes.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
